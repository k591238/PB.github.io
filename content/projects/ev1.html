《創作過程中的思考：從觀察到呈現》

Artist Talk & TouchDesigner Workshop TA

Events:

PXP 2026 — Perspectives × Public Visuals Audiovisual Arts Festival

Date: 2026 / 08 / 22–23

Venue: Taipei Music Center Live House D 台北流行音樂中心 Live House D

Speaker / Workshop TA：吳承儒 Wu Cheng-Ru (PB)

在 PXP 2026 音像藝術節中，吳承儒(PB)受邀擔任藝術家短講講者，以《創作過程中的思考：從觀察到呈現》為題，分享自己從《視感認知 Mind Fluid》、《認夢 Dream Percept》、《聽說 Whisper》到《繫 Sync》等作品的創作脈絡，談如何從日常觀察、媒材本質與數位訊號的失真出發，逐步發展成現場音像演出與展覽呈現。同場次中，他也擔任 TouchDesigner Workshop 的工作坊助教，協助講師 Ben Voigt 帶領學員進行即時影像的實作練習。


At PXP 2026 Audiovisual Arts Festival, Wu Cheng-Ru (PB) was invited to give an artist talk titled "Thinking in the Creative Process: From Observation to Presentation," tracing the development of his works — from "Mind Fluid" and "Dream Percept" to "Whisper" and "Sync" — and discussing how everyday observation, the nature of materials, and the distortion of digital signals evolve into live audiovisual performances and exhibitions. During the same program, he also served as a teaching assistant for the TouchDesigner workshop led by Ben Voigt, supporting participants through hands-on real-time visual exercises.
