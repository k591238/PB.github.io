《繫 Sync》 展覽版 Exhibition Ver.

《繫 Sync》回應空間的既有結構與語彙，透過音像技術生成沉浸式現場，打造一座異質化的感知風景。展覽中，藝術家將影像、聲響與空間動線交織，使觀眾在移動與觀看之間，感受影像與身體的相互牽引。


不同畫面的分割、交錯與重組，形成連結與斷裂並存的敘事節奏，也讓展覽成為一處關於感知、身體與空間關係的現場實驗。


Wu Cheng-Ru’s solo exhibition Sync responds to the existing structure and spatial language of the site, creating a heterogeneous landscape of perception through audiovisual techniques.


Moving images, sound, and spatial arrangement intertwine, inviting viewers to experience the connection between image and body. Through fragmented visuals and shifting rhythms, the exhibition explores narratives of connection and rupture within space.

《繫 Sync》 閉幕演出 Performance Ver.

本次演出作為展覽的延伸與回應，將主軸聚焦於藝術家自身「接收外在資訊後，內在狀態變化的過程」。


每一次聲音、影像與空間的變化，觀眾的介入、觀看方式與感知狀態，都將共同成為演出的一部分，那些隨機發生的片刻，不會被排除在作品之外，而是被轉化為作品本身，呈現機遇性與無法再現的當下。


Serving as an extension and response to the exhibition, this performance centers on the artist’s own ”process of internal transformation after receiving external information.“


Every shift in sound, image, and space—along with the audience‘s intervention, gaze, and perceptual state—co-creates the performance. These chance moments are not excluded from the piece; instead, they are transformed into the work itself, manifesting the fortuitous and irreproducible present.
