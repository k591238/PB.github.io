《 聽 說 Whisper 》

Sound Installation, Sound resample, 8 channel

Exhibition

2024 關渡光藝術節X後花園實驗場跨域展演《燒聲》

2024 Kuan Du Light Art Festival

在許多未知的背後，獲得資訊的管道經常是如此模糊，甚至內容常帶有各種謬誤。《聽說》所傳遞的聲音亦是如此，資訊來源只有來自水管內的聲音，卻無法確定其真實性和具體內容為何。


Behind many unknowns, the channels for obtaining information are often so unclear, and the content frequently contains various fallacies. The voices conveyed through "Whisper" are no different. The source of the information comes only from the sounds within the pipes, but its authenticity and specific content remain uncertain.
