《Thiann》聽

Audiovisual Live Performance · 15 min

Performance:

Sound Campus 26 – Listening Underground, Ars Electronica Festival Linz

Date: 2026/09/09

Venue: Kunstuniversität Linz（林茲藝術與設計大學）, Hauptplatz 6, Linz, Austria

Sound / Visual：吳承儒 Wu Cheng-Ru (PB)

關於藝術家 About the Artist

吳承儒(PB)是一位來自台灣的新媒體藝術家，專注於音像演出創作。他透過聲音實驗、即時影像與數位訊號的整合，藉由操縱訊號傳遞過程中的摩擦與失真，探討人與人之間的關係與認知落差。


Wu Cheng-Ru is a Taiwan-based new media artist specializing in audiovisual performance. Through sound experimentation, real-time visuals, and digital signal integration, his work explores human relationships and cognitive gaps by manipulating the friction and distortions within signal transmission.



作品簡介 About this Project

「Thiann」是台語中「聽」的意思。演出內容透過藝術家收集在臺灣的日常聲響與畫面，由鄉村攤販的廣播或車輛的限速提示音等，透過數位的重混與調變，讓臺灣日常的印象透過聲景(Soundscape)再現，並透過音像演出詮釋藝術家在臺灣生活的觀察與狀態。


"Thiann" derives from the Taiwanese word for "listen." Through a collection of everyday sounds and visuals gathered across Taiwan—such as broadcasts from rural street vendors and vehicle speed alerts—the performance digitally remixes and modulates these elements to reconstruct daily Taiwanese impressions into a soundscape, interpreting the artist's personal observations and lived reality in Taiwan through live audiovisual performance.
