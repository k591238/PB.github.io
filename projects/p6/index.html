視感認知 Visual Cognition

Audio-Visual Live Performance · 15 min

Performance:

《新聲複樂之夜》現場電聲 X 音像演出 — C-LAB 臺灣聲響實驗室 × 2026 新響藝術季

Date: 2026 / 08 / 21 (Fri) 19:30–20:30

Venue: 新竹縣政府文化局 實驗劇場

在數位影像當中，我們透過認知上的投射，來獲得對此影像的認知，而這些數位影像在電腦當中的呈現卻只是像素(Pixel)上的座標，也因此數位影像的觀看中，我們經常遊蕩於抽象模糊與寫實具體之間，透過帶入自身的經驗來觀看數位影像，並得出我們對此影像的認知為何。《視感認知》於 2026 新響藝術季《新聲複樂之夜》中，以 15 分鐘現場電聲 X 音像演出的形式重新詮釋，由 C-LAB 臺灣聲響實驗室策劃，在新竹縣政府文化局實驗劇場演出。


In digital imagery, our perception comes from projecting meaning onto what we see — yet within a computer, these images are nothing more than coordinates on a grid of pixels. Viewing digital images, we constantly drift between abstract ambiguity and concrete realism, drawing on our own experience to construct what we perceive. "Visual Cognition" was reinterpreted as a 15-minute live electronic and audiovisual performance at "A Night of New Sound Remix," part of the 2026 Xīn Xiǎng Arts Festival, curated by C-LAB Taiwan Sound Lab, and performed at the Experimental Theater of the Hsinchu County Government Cultural Affairs Bureau.
