WU CHENG-RU

吳承儒  ·  PB / Pen_bit

New Media Artist  ·  Interactive Developer  ·  Audiovisual Artist

CV PDF ↓

A new media artist from Chiayi, Taiwan.

His work spans interdisciplinary art and audiovisual performance, built around sound experimentation, real-time image generation, and digital signal integration.

At its core, his practice focuses on human relationships, using the distortion that occurs as digital signals are transmitted to explore cognitive differences in visual observation.

He is actively engaged across interdisciplinary art, theater, dance, and sound installation, continually expanding the possibilities of new media art.

He has taken part in exhibitions and performances including Dream Percept at C-LAB, FUTURE VISION LAB, Whisper at the Kuan Du Light Art Festival, and Glub Glub at Nuit Blanche Taipei.

來自臺灣嘉義的新媒體藝術家。

創作多涵蓋跨領域藝術與音像演出（Audiovisual performance），並以聲響實驗、即時影像生成與數位訊號整合為發展核心。

創作核心聚焦於人文關係，透過數位訊號傳遞過程中的失真，探討視覺觀察中的認知差異。

積極投入跨領域藝術、劇場、舞蹈與聲音裝置等多元領域，持續拓展新媒體藝術的創新可能性。

曾參與 C-LAB《認夢》、FUTURE VISION LAB《未來視覺派對》、關渡光藝術節《聽說》及白晝之夜《鹹淡適中》等展演計畫。
